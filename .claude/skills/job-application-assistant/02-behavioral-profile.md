## Overview
Pavan's 16Personalities result is **ENTJ-A "Commander"** — a decisive, strategically-minded leader pattern. He is energized by driving toward goals, thinks in big-picture systems, decides with logic, works in a structured and planful way, and is self-assured under pressure. This aligns closely with the CV evidence: building systems from scratch, owning them end-to-end, and framing work in measurable results.

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Drive & leadership:** Natural ownership of outcomes; takes charge and pushes work to completion (ENTJ core; matches "built from scratch / owned end-to-end").
- **Strategic systems thinking:** Intuitive + Thinking — sees the architecture and long-term shape of a problem, which fits his stated pull toward large-scale system design.
- **Decisive, organized execution:** Very high Judging — plans, prioritizes, and drives to closure rather than leaving things open.
- **Confidence under pressure:** Assertive identity — stays steady during load tests, incidents, and scaling crunches.

## How You Work Best

- Fast-moving, merit-based, high-performance environments where competence and results are recognized
- Room to lead, make decisions, and drive technical direction
- Efficiency valued; low tolerance for unnecessary bureaucracy or indecision

## Caption
I cloned this open source AI job search agent (runs on Claude Code) thinking it would help me land a job, and after going through it I got to know it was built for the Danish market. They said it works for any country but honestly I didn't think it would for us.

So I tried pointing it at Naukri, LinkedIn and Indeed, got no results, they don't allow scraping 😕 Claude suggested a Playwright script but that risks a LinkedIn ban, so I didn't risk my account. Instead I found some remote sites that allow scraping, kept only the roles open to anywhere in the world, added a filter for the most recent posts, and it worked, I got some posts that are somewhat related 🙂

The application engine is MadsLorentzen's framework, I just changed the search side for India. It's on my GitHub, try it out and let me know how it feels 🙌
